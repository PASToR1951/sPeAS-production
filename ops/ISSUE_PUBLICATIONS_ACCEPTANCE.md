## Named manuscript regressions

The four named abstract regressions pass on their actual preliminary sources: Masacayan 4–5, Calumpang 3–4, Ablay 5–6 and Epan 8–9. Their final continuation pages were visually checked. Source extraction found and fixed repeated-header/page-number contamination rather than relying solely on synthetic tests.

The unchanged Word renderer still reproduces Santos's blank permission-letter pages and Empeso's clipped annex scans. Embedded originals are retained, and a private sizing experiment did not establish a reliable repair. These known pre-existing layout cases require corrected prepared PDFs and review/hold; this issue feature does not automatically rewrite source documents. See the source report for the exact component-level evidence and its limits: historical consolidated page numbers are not asserted to equal newly converted component-local page numbers.
